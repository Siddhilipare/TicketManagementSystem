SP for Master Detail transaction thir flow and use

You can explain it to your ma'am like this:

### **1. `sp_ShowTicketList`**

* Used to display the **Ticket List page**.
* It fetches the booking summary such as customer name, movie, cinema hall, screen, total tickets, show time, and total amount.

---

### **2. `sp_GetTicketByID`**

* Called when the user clicks **Edit** on a ticket.
* It retrieves the complete ticket information, including the **master details** (customer, movie, show details) and the **booked seat details**.

---

### **3. `sp_GetTicketDropdownData`**

* Used for the **dependent dropdowns** in the Add/Edit Ticket form.
* It loads Cinema Halls, Screens, Movies, Show Dates, and Show Times based on the user's previous selection.

---

### **4. `sp_UpdateTicket`**

* Used to save changes made to the **master information** of a ticket.
* It updates the customer name and changes the booking to the selected show (movie, hall, screen, date, and time).

---

### **5. `sp_GetSeatMapForEdit`**

* Used when opening the **seat selection** during ticket editing.
* It returns all seats of the selected screen and indicates which seats are **available**, **already booked**, and **currently selected** for that ticket.

---

### **6. `sp_SaveTicketSeats`**

* Used after the user saves the updated seat selection.
* It inserts new seats, removes deselected seats, prevents double booking, and recalculates the total ticket count and payment amount.

---

## **Overall Flow (30-second explanation)**

1. **`sp_ShowTicketList`** → Displays all booked tickets.
2. **`sp_GetTicketByID`** → Loads the selected ticket for editing.
3. **`sp_GetTicketDropdownData`** → Fills the cascading dropdowns (Hall → Screen → Movie → Date → Time).
4. **`sp_GetSeatMapForEdit`** → Displays the seat layout with booked and available seats.
5. **`sp_UpdateTicket`** → Saves changes to the ticket's master information.
6. **`sp_SaveTicketSeats`** → Saves the updated seat selection and recalculates the ticket count and payment.

This gives a clear, interview-style explanation of how the stored procedures work together in the application.
